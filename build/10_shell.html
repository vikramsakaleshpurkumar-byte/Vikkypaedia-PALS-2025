Four short steps. It takes about a minute, and it decides how the next twenty-eight hours are shaped for you.

  • 1
    Mastery, not completion. There is no “mark as read” button anywhere. A unit is mastered when both its checkpoint questions are currently correct — and it can be lost again.
  • 2
    Parts unlock in order. Part B opens when every unit in Part A is mastered. Part A is open now.
  • 3
    Hints before answers. Stuck? Take a nudge, then a structured hint. Using one still counts — the item simply comes back sooner.
  • 4

How this module works

  • Mastery, not completion. There is no “mark as read” button anywhere. A unit is mastered when both its checkpoint questions are currently answered correctly.
  • Parts unlock in order. Part B opens when every unit in Part A is mastered, and so on. Part A is open now.
  • Hints before answers. Stuck on a question? Take a nudge, then a structured hint. Using a hint still counts — the item simply comes back sooner.
  • Spaced retrieval. Passed items return at 1, 3, 7, 21 and 60 days. A miss returns in 10 minutes.
  • Remediation is built in. Every wrong answer opens the full rationale, points at the exact section, and re-queues the item.

Your privacy

  • Everything you do stays in this browser on this device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no telemetry.
  • Nothing is transmitted anywhere. The file works with aeroplane mode on.
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 is satisfied here by collecting nothing at all.
  • You can erase all progress with one click at the bottom of the final assessment.

Clinical safety — read this once

This module is education, not a protocol, and not certification to practise. It is an original work written from the 2025 American Heart Association and American Academy of Pediatrics Guidelines for Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation and Emergency Cardiovascular Care (Part 6: Pediatric Basic Life Support; Part 8: Pediatric Advanced Life Support) and ILCOR 2025. It is not the AHA PALS course, does not reproduce the AHA provider manual, confers no PALS provider status, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the AHA or AAP. PALS® is a trademark of the American Heart Association.

Verify every dose, threshold and device setting against your institution’s current protocol and the manufacturer’s instructions before use in a patient. Appendix E (drug and equipment annex) and Appendix F (references) are never locked — they are open from the first minute, because clinical safety material behind a quiz is a patient-safety problem.
